## Decisions & findings
Facts only; interpretation is left to the reader.
- Verified: native-resolution rows are within 0.7 dB of the input in PSNR (CIs in the table); every x4 + bicubic row (see table) is above every native row.
- Verified: the harness reproduces the published numbers on the original images (input, Restormer, DRBNet, IFAN within ~0.06 dB / 0.003 SSIM). Our raw-built renderings change the numbers by -0.58..+0.16 dB per model and reorder the models.
- Surprising / broken: (1) the handoff-2 claim "DP maps missing for val and train" made in this handoff's first data check was wrong — they exist for all splits (test 76/76, val 74/74, train 350/350); only the test maps are linked into the native-set tree. (2) DATSR is much faster than the handoff-2 estimate (80-93 s vs >15 min per image); the earlier number was measured on a heavily shared GPU.
- Terminology: b0 = focal plane (in focus), b3 = most defocused, as in the runbook.
