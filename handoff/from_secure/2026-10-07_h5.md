# Handoff 5 — training setup validation (V1–V6), real anchors, cache, retrieval, first overfit — 2026-10-07

## TL;DR
- The training setup validates through the step-0 check: train / val inputs and DP maps link and verify (350 / 74 scenes, no scene overlap with the 76 test scenes), offline weights load (DINOv2-L, VGG19, HAT-L), real anchors are scored on train and val, the cache is built (train 143 GB, val 31 GB), and the untrained model (HAT-L x4 + exact anchor lock) is reproduced at step 0 (val PSNR 24.38, -0.42 vs bicubic; x4 PSNR exact).
- **V6 (overfit) is not passed.** With the default loss (L1 + 0.1 x contextual) val PSNR on the two overfit scenes rises only 32.21 -> 32.94 and plateaus; with L1 only and no EMA it reaches 33.84-33.85 = bicubic (33.88) within 500 steps and stays there to step 1500. The gradient norm shows the contextual term supplies almost all of the gradient (0.4-0.7 vs 0.002-0.02 for L1 alone).
- Retrieval: DINOv2 recall@16 of the oracle top-1 is 0.047 (train) / 0.045 (val) vs pixel features 0.039 / 0.035 and chance 0.010; the paired difference is significant on train only (+0.008, CI [+0.002, +0.014]), not on val. Visually, retrieval finds the right material for texture-like regions (leaf surface) and fails on edges and structures; the oracle (a high-band spectrum match) does not look like the target.
- Not done: V1 DP-map overlay check, V2 token-grid timing, V7 smoke throughput (workers 4/8/12), V8 / V9, and all full training runs. No test-set number was produced; the test set was never used.

For a leaf edge against a dark background, a stem and a dark bead cluster the top-4 are flat dark-green or dark patches without the edge or structure (one top-4 hit has a dark rounded blob on a light background).
  - Fence / peeling paint / concrete scene (1P0A1014, median recall 0.029): for concrete with fence wire the top-4 are pinkish diagonal mesh textures (plausible); for peeling yellow paint and a dark vertical edge they are unrelated structure (a dark rock-like blob, generic grey surfaces, dark flat patches).

## Decisions & findings
- **Verified:** anchor lock is exact (x4 PSNR constant in every row); the untrained model reproduces HAT-L x4 below bicubic; Restormer / DRBNet anchors on train are optimistic by 0.75-1.8 dB PSNR relative to val / test; a missing DP-map link and a missing VGG file were the only data-side blockers.
- **Verified:** HAT-L at batch 4 and 512 px tiles ran out of memory (79 GB) without activation checkpointing; with it, 11.95 GB.
- **Hypotheses (not tested):** the contextual term dominates the gradient and is the reason PSNR does not rise (supported by the gradient norms and the L1-only run, but the two runs differ in two settings, loss and EMA); the L1-only plateau at bicubic may be a model / data limit (two defocused scenes, no detail in the input) and not an optimisation problem.
- **Claim to adjust:** DINOv2 retrieval is only modestly better than pixel features (recall@16 1.2x; significant on train, not on val), so "DINOv2 retrieves better than pixels" should not be stated strongly; and recall@16 should not be read as retrieval quality (the oracle is a spectral match, not an appearance match).

## Open questions / needs from outside
