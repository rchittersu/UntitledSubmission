## Decisions & findings
- **Verified (on these 6 tiles):** the highband baseline beats both diffusion models on PSNR, LPIPS and DISTS; the diffusion models are below bicubic in every bin raw; the anchor lock recovers ~1 dB in b0 but not the full gap.
- **Hypothesis:** ReFIR's reference has no effect in this setup (self vs retrieved < 0.1 dB; SeeSR with and without reference identical). Needs an adapter check (is the reference passed and used in the attention path?) before any conclusion about references.
- **Visual, two tiles (describe only, one crop each):** tile 1 (sand/wood ridges, b0): highband recovers the ridge edges closest to the target; ReFIR and SeeSR are smooth and lose grain, with some smearing lower right. Tile 2 (red script, b2): stroke shapes survive in ReFIR and SeeSR, paper texture is lost, and faint diagonal streaks appear next to strokes in the lower half of the diffusion outputs (possibly ringing or invented lines; needs a pixel-level check).
- **Deviation:** scene 1P0A1046 is from the test set; used at the user's explicit request for baseline benchmarking only; nothing was tuned on it. Its numbers should not be used as validation evidence.
