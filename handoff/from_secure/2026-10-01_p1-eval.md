## Standardization check — same 37 indoor scenes, 1680x1120, whole image, fp32 (2026-10-01)

A = author-shared pairs (HF copy, 8-bit targets, no masks/crop). B = our 4x area downsample of the
developed native images (registered targets, masks, 16 px border crop). 4090, 37 images each.

| Model | A PSNR↑ | B PSNR↑ | A SSIM↑ | B SSIM↑ | A MAE↓ | B MAE↓ | A LPIPS↓ | B LPIPS↓ | s/img | peak GB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restormer | 28.90 | 26.53 | 0.883 | 0.863 | 0.025 | 0.036 | 0.142 | 0.149 | 1.7 | 16.0 |
| LaKDNet-L | **29.05** | 26.49 | 0.877 | 0.848 | 0.024 | 0.036 | 0.116 | 0.131 | 1.9 | 14.7 |
| DRBNet | 28.53 | **26.69** | 0.864 | 0.859 | 0.026 | 0.037 | 0.137 | 0.121 | 0.17 | 6.0 |
| Bokehlicious | 28.77 | 26.09 | 0.871 | **0.865** | 0.027 | 0.041 | 0.128 | **0.107** | 1.0 | 3.9 |

Observations: B is 1.9-2.7 dB below A for every model (colour/tone residual of our development, D3, plus
registration of targets); the *ranking changes* (LaKDNet best on A, DRBNet on B; LPIPS ranking flips for Bokehlicious).
Absolute numbers on B are therefore not comparable to published DPDD numbers; relative comparisons across scales
within B are. Not yet separated: contribution of colour mapping vs. registration vs. 4x filter (todo: evaluate B
predictions against A targets).

## Concerns about the evaluation and proposed improvements  [DRAFT — finalize with the full P1 tables]

Goal: make the evaluation credible on its own before any method work. Ordered by how much each item can change a conclusion.
Status tags: **verified** (measured here) / **hypothesis** (not yet tested).

### A. Data and ground truth
1. **Only 37 test pairs, all indoor** (**verified**): raw is available for the indoor half only (37 of 76 test scenes). Scene diversity is low; per-image std is large (Restormer whole-image PSNR on the 37 scenes ranges 21–32 dB). → Report paired per-image deltas with bootstrap CIs, not only means; obtain the outdoor raws to reach 76.
2. **Native images are developed by us, not Canon DPP** (**verified**): after the fitted global colour/tone map, held-out agreement with the official PNGs is 28.7 dB (test) at 1/4 scale; local DPP processing (tone/sharpening/noise reduction) is not reproduced. Consequence: absolute PSNR/SSIM are not comparable to published DPDD numbers (A vs B: −1.9 to −2.7 dB for every model, and model ranking changes). → Use B-type numbers only comparatively; try a local (spatially varying / learned) mapping or the authors' DPP settings; check whether the ranking flip (LaKDNet best on A, DRBNet on B) survives a better development.
3. **Misalignment between the f/4 and f/22 captures is large at native resolution** (**verified**): corner shift median 5.6 px, p90 25 px, max 79 px; 12/37 images > 10 px, 2 with ECC < 0.9 (`1P0A2350`, 0.892). Registration is a single global homography; local misalignment (parallax, focus breathing, moving content) remains and penalizes PSNR/SSIM in a method-independent but image-dependent way. → Report registered-fraction masks, add a local flow-based residual check, and show results with the worst images excluded as a sensitivity analysis (never silently).
4. **Diffraction-limited f/22 GT** (**hypothesis for magnitude**): PM-PSNR is computed with a theoretical Airy PSF; we have not yet verified that the residual GT blur matches it (lens aberrations may add more). PM-PSNR > PSNR in every row so far (+0.1…+0.3 dB), i.e. the effect is small at 1/4 scale, as predicted. → Estimate the actual GT MTF from slanted edges / sharp regions before claiming PM as primary.
5. **Which downscale filter made the official 1680×1120 is still unknown** (D4): all filters within 1 dB because of D3. → Resolve with a colour-independent test (e.g. aliasing energy above Nyquist, or gradient-domain comparison), or obtain a raw→PNG pair processed by the authors.
6. **D2: reproduction is 0.03 dB above the paper's PSNR** (**verified**), LPIPS 0.004 lower. Tolerance in the runbook was 0.01. Possible causes (**hypotheses**): HF copy of the data differs from the authors' files; PSNR averaging (per-image mean vs. pooled); LPIPS backbone/version. → Compare with Restormer's own evaluation script on the same outputs; log both.

### B. Metrics
7. **Missing metric families** (**verified from code state**): semantic/texture consistency (DINOv2-matched patch pairs), blur-level (CoC-bin) stratification from the dual-pixel views (`test_l`/`test_r` not downloaded), no-reference metrics (MUSIQ/MANIQA/CLIPIQA) and human study are not run. P1 only covers fidelity, PM, high-band, LPIPS/DISTS, drift, seam, stats.
8. **Seam ratio definition** (**hypothesis**): ratio of error on tile boundaries vs. control lines is ≈1.1–1.4 for all models; no baseline for what "no seams" looks like. → Add a synthetic control (same model, whole-image at a size that fits) to calibrate; report the seam metric only where the model is actually tiled.
9. **High-band NMSE sign convention**: values change from −2.3 dB (×4) to +1.9 dB (native) for Restormer; > 0 dB means worse than outputting the blurry input's high band. Needs a one-line definition and a reference row (the blurry input) in every table — the `references` group provides it.
10. **LPIPS/DISTS are computed on 1024-px tiles** (`fr_tile`) and averaged; this is not the same as full-image LPIPS at 1680×1120 (D2). State it in the paper and keep the setting fixed.
11. **Statistics**: n = 37, one run per setting, deterministic inference; no seeds to vary, but image-level bootstrap CIs are needed before ranking methods that differ by < 0.3 dB.

### C. Protocol and baselines
12. **Baselines were trained at ≤ 1680×1120**, so tile size/overlap/blending are a free choice that changes results (512 vs 1024 in G1; overlap 64, linear blend). Only these two settings are covered; no TLC/other blending ablation yet. → Add an overlap/blend ablation on one model.
13. **Baseline coverage**: IFAN missing (weights blocked by the proxy); no NAFNet/FFTFormer/EVSSM-type recent model; G2 has no generative SR (StableSR/SUPIR/OSEDiff) and G3 (guided upsampling) is not implemented. The G2 conclusion "upsampling after low-res deblur loses detail" is currently only supported by bicubic/SwinIR.
14. **Precision/hardware**: reported numbers are fp32; timings were measured on a shared 8-GPU node (device string only reports "CUDA GPU") and a 24 GB desktop GPU; the 1024-tile native step runs out of memory on 24 GB at `tile_batch` 8. Timings in the paper must come from one named GPU with exclusive access.

### D. Process / reproducibility
15. Third-party weights are verified by sha256 (runbook list); DRBNet and the data copies came via manual transfer. Metric backbones (AlexNet/VGG16, LPIPS/DISTS heads) must be present offline on the compute node — missing files silently hang the run (cost us ~15 min once).
16. Raw→PNG development script, fitted colour map and registration CSV are the only artefacts that define the benchmark; they should be versioned and described in the paper's protocol section (`code/scripts/develop_raw.py`, `register_pairs.py`).
