## Results (observations from the crops; 3 comparison windows + 4 texture windows, not a statistic)
- **Comparison (Fig. 6).** Native inference leaves the input as blurry as it is. x4 deblur + bicubic and + HAT-L recover edges and legible text; the DP composite looks like bicubic. SwinIR-real adds contrast and alters glyph shapes. OSEDiff rewrites the poster caption into different glyphs, changes the shape of the locks and adds banding in the dark scene. S3Diff is sharper but also changes glyph shapes and adds grain in the dark scene. VOSR2 stays close to bicubic with legible text.
- **Same texture in focus and defocused (Fig. 5).** In 1P0A1046 (printed script on fabric) and 1P0A1112 (woven fabric) the same material is sharp in one part of the image and defocused in another. In the defocused windows the input and native inference stay blurred; x4 deblur + bicubic / HAT-L recover the stroke geometry but not the fine weave; OSEDiff smooths the strokes and replaces the in-focus weave by a regular mesh; S3Diff adds weave-like texture to the defocused fabric; VOSR2 adds a regular grid at the right edge of the defocused weave. The in-focus windows contain the texture that exemplar transfer would copy.
- **Bins (Fig. 3).** Flat, low-texture regions get low dual-pixel confidence and can fall in b0 even when defocused (the plain wall in 1P0A1002); not quantified.
