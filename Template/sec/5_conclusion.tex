\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% =====================================================================
Defocus deblurring at the resolution at which photographs are taken is not solved by existing methods:
run natively they barely change the image, and run at low resolution they need an upsampler that either
loses or invents detail. On the native DPDD benchmark every existing approach we evaluated lies on a
single fidelity--perception trade-off, with bicubic upsampling of a low-resolution result as the
fidelity ceiling and in-focus damage as the price of generative detail. Because defocus varies across the image, the photograph itself holds much of
the detail that is missing, in its in-focus regions. \method uses this evidence to lift any
